Questions before you buy

When does my time count?

Only while your camera is streaming to the studio. Setting up, choosing a face and pausing are free.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. A laptop with a webcam and Chrome or Edge is enough; the face swap runs on our servers.

What internet speed do I need?

A steady upload of about 8 Mbit/s for full HD. On slower links, lower the picture size in the studio.

Can I use it in Zoom, Meet or Teams?

Yes, through OBS Virtual Camera: capture the studio window in OBS, start the virtual camera and choose it in your call app.

Do hours expire?

Hours stay on your account unless the package says "use within" a number of days. An invite code has to be redeemed before the date shown on the invite.
