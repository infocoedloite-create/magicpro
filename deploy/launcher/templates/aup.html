Acceptable use policy

{{ S.brand }} changes the face in your live video. These rules protect the people whose faces appear and the people who watch. By using {{ S.brand }} you agree to them.

Faces you may use

  • Your own face.
  • Another person's face only with their prior, written consent for this use. Keep that consent and show it to us if we ask.
  • Public figures, celebrities, politicians and officials only with their written consent, the same as anyone else.
  • Never the face of a child.

What you must not do

  • Impersonate a real person, or make viewers believe a real person said or did something they didn't.
  • Commit or prepare fraud, scams, identity theft or to get past identity or age checks (for example "video KYC").
  • Create sexual, nude or intimate content of anyone, or any content involving minors.
  • Harass, bully, threaten, defame or humiliate anyone.
  • Create deceptive political content, fake news or misinformation.
  • Remove, hide or crop out the "AI face swap" label, or present the output as unaltered video.
  • Break any law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

Labelling

Every picture {{ S.brand }} produces carries a visible "AI face swap" label. When you publish or stream it, tell your audience that the video is altered.

What we do

We may review reports, pause or close accounts that break these rules without refund of unused hours, remove uploaded photos, and cooperate with law enforcement where the law requires it.

Report misuse

If you see {{ S.brand }} used with your face without consent, or used to deceive, {% if S.support_email %}write to {{ S.support_email }}{% else %}contact us through the address on this site{% endif %}. We act on reports within the time the law requires.
